# Skill: Earnings Analysis

## Goal

Identify what materially changed in a company's earnings report or guidance.

## Focus On

- Revenue vs expectations
- EPS vs expectations
- Guidance
- Margins
- Growth
- Key business segments
- Management outlook
- Major demand changes

Prioritize forward guidance over headline beats or misses.

## Output

Return:

- importance: 1–10
- sentiment
- affected
- key_change
- why_read
