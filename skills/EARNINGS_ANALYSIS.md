Skill: Earnings Analysis

Goal

Identify material reported results, outlook changes, and uncertainties in supplied earnings evidence.

Focus

Revenue, EPS, outlook, margins, growth, segments, management commentary, and demand. Consider forward guidance alongside results, not as an automatic override.

Evidence Checks

Preserve fiscal/calendar periods, currencies, units, reported/adjusted measures, and actual-versus-estimated status.

Compare results with consensus only when matching expectations and measurement bases are supplied, or explicitly attribute a source's reported comparison. Otherwise, a beat/miss is not established.

Call guidance raised/lowered only with supplied prior guidance or explicit reporting of that change. Do not infer a raise from a large outlook number.

Separate company guidance from analyst targets and management market-size forecasts. Do not relabel industry opportunity as company revenue guidance.

Do not equate backlog/orders with recognized revenue or invent cancellation terms, conversion timing, or customer commitments.

Use supplied Python calculations or quoted source figures for growth, margins, and surprises. Do not calculate missing comparisons.

Weigh conflicting signals and context. Strong revenue alone does not establish improving profitability; an earnings beat alone does not imply a stock-price gain.

Output

Retain importance (integer 1–10), sentiment, affected, key_change, and why_read.

7+ requires concrete material earnings/outlook information; vague previews and unsupported projections stay lower. Score magnitude against supplied business context rather than absolute numbers alone.

key_change identifies the principal supported development, preserving attribution and period. why_read is at most one sentence about its significance or a conditional implication. Mention a missing comparison when necessary instead of filling the gap.
