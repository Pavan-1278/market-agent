# Skill: Macro Analysis

## Goal

Determine how a macro development could affect US equities and the watchlist.

## Focus On

- Federal Reserve
- Interest rates
- CPI
- PCE
- Jobs
- GDP
- Treasury yields
- VIX
- Oil
- Geopolitics
- Semiconductor restrictions

## Reasoning

Identify what changed versus expectations when that information is available.

Consider potential effects on:

- SPY
- QQQ
- Growth stocks
- Semiconductors
- AI stocks

Do not assume every macro event materially affects every stock.

## Output

Return:

- importance: 1–10
- sentiment
- affected
- market_impact
- why_read
